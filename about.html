About

Born in Kathmandu, fed by its kitchens

We started with one delivery bike and one momo shop in 2019. Today we partner with 500+ kitchens across the Valley.

Our story

A Valley company, not a franchise

Most food delivery is built for big cities and chain kitchens. We built GoodForFoods for family shops, thakali houses and neighbourhood legends — and for neighbours who want them delivered hot.

  • Founded in Kathmandu, 2019
  • 500+ partner kitchens across three districts
  • Riders employed, insured and trained in house
  • Zero-commission onboarding for small kitchens

How we work

The GoodForFoods loop

  1. Kitchens apply

    We audit hygiene, taste-test and agree honest prep times.

  2. Menus go live

    Kitchens control their own menu, prices and offers from a simple tablet app.

  3. Orders flow

    Riders pick up, track and deliver with live updates to your phone.

  4. Quality loops back

    Every rating, complaint and refund feeds back into kitchen support.
